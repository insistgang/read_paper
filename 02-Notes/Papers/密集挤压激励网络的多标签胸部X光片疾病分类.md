## 摘要

**英文原摘要（据 PDF 提取文本整理）**：

> Chest X-ray is a common and economical method for diagnosing a variety of chest diseases. However, the locations and lesion-region sizes of different diseases differ greatly on chest X-ray films, and the textures present diversity, which is a major challenge for the chest disease classification task. In addition, the class imbalance of the sample data further increases the difficulty of the task. To address the above challenges and the problem that the recognition accuracy of current algorithms remains to be improved, this paper proposes a multilabel chest disease classification algorithm based on a dense squeeze-and-excitation network using deep learning. The squeeze-and-excitation (SE) module is added to the densely connected network in a densely connected manner as a feature-channel high-attention module, so as to enhance the transmission of information useful for correct disease judgment while suppressing the transmission of useless information; an asymmetric convolution block is used to enhance the feature-extraction ability of the network; and a focal loss function is adopted to increase the loss weight of hard-to-recognize diseases and reduce the loss weight of easy-to-recognize diseases, thereby strengthening the network's learning of hard samples. Experiments on the ChestX-ray14 dataset show that, for the classification of 14 chest diseases, the proposed algorithm improves over 9 classic and advanced algorithms, with an average AUC (area under curve) reaching 0.8302. In addition, the lesion regions on which the model bases its diagnosis are visualized, and the results further demonstrate the effectiveness of the model.

## 方法

- **整体框架**：以密集连接网络（DenseNet）为骨干，将 SE 模块以密集连接的方式插入，构成"密集挤压激励"特征提取结构。
- **输入输出**：输入为胸部 X 光片图像；输出为对 14 种胸部疾病的多标签判断（每种疾病是否存在）。
