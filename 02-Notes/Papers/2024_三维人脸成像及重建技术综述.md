## 摘要

**英文原摘要（据提取文本整理，已修复空格/连字符、剔除杂入的版式文字）：**

> As the breakthrough technology of artificial intelligence (AI) in the big data era, deep learning (DL) has prompted the renewed upsurge of face technology. Powered by rapid developments of new technologies, such as three-dimensional (3D) vision measurement, image processing chips, and DL models, 3D vision transformed into a key supporting technology in AI, visual reality, etc. The studies and applications of 3D facial imaging and reconstruction technologies have achieved important breakthroughs. 3D face data represent exact multidimensional facial attributes on account of rich visual information, such as texture, shape, space, etc. Moreover, 3D face data shows robust changes in large occlusions, expressions, and poses and increases the difficulty of forgery attack. Therefore, 3D face imaging and reconstruction effectively promote realistic "virtual digital human" reconstruction and rendering. In addition, these processes contribute to the improved security of the face system. In this paper, we comprehensively study the 3D face imaging technology and reconstruction models. The 3D face reconstruction methods based on DL are systematically and deeply analyzed. From the perspective of input resources, 3D face reconstruction methods based on DL are categorized into monocular, multiview, video and audio reconstruction methods. The DL network structure is categorized into general deep convolutional neural network (such as ResNet, U-Net, and Autoencoder), generative adversarial networks (GANs), implicit neural representation (INR) (such as NeRF and signed distance functions (SDF)), and Transformer. 3DMM and FLAME are widely used 3D face representation models. The StyleGAN model gives excellent performance in recovering high-quality face texture. The NeRF model plays an important role in the reconstruction of accurate head avatars, and its combination with GAN shows great potential in high-fidelity geometry and realistic rendering. The Transformer model is mainly used in audio-driven 3D face reconstruction. We proposed a comprehensive and systematic review and covered classical technologies and studies on 3D face imaging and reconstruction in the last five years.

## 研究背景与动机

- **为什么做**：相比二维人脸，三维人脸具有独特优势——①包含纹理、形状、空间等更丰富的视觉信息，可多维度表征人脸属性；②对光照、遮挡、表情、姿态等变化更鲁棒，增大伪造攻击难度，提升系统精度与安全性；③得益于新型光电成像、三维视觉测量技术发展，数据获取和模型创新不再是制约因素，三维人脸成为计算机视觉重要研究方向（p.2442）。应用上聚焦"虚拟数字人"与智能移动终端轻量化人工智能（Tiny AI）。
- **面临的难题与挑战**（引言明确列出三点，p.2442）：
    1.  **数据瓶颈**：数据集规模和多样性是深度模型基础，但大规模高质量三维人脸数据库相对缺乏，现有公开库在规模、表情、姿态和遮挡覆盖上仍有局限。
    2.  **表情与遮挡**：是三维人脸研究的两大难题，尤其在基于大规模野外（"in-the-wild"）二维图像重建时，复杂表情运动与遮挡异常区域严重影响精度。
    3.  **移动端轻量化**：基于智能移动成像设备采集与重建、将深度模型计算轻量化，是更大的挑战，也是推广应用的关键。
- **与已有综述的差异**：引言系统对比了既有综述（p.2442）——Zollhöfer 等(2018)总结单目/RGB-D 的人脸重建、跟踪与重演；Egger 等(2020)介绍经典 3DMM；Sharma 和 Kumar(2022)将重建分为 3DMM、对极几何、明暗恢复形状、单样本学习、深度学习与混合学习 6 类；何嘉玉等(2022)将单目重建分为 CNN/GCN/自编码器/GAN 四类；王静婷和李慧斌(2023)分为隐空间编码与显空间回归两类。本综述强调从**输入数据源**角度分类，并将成像技术与重建算法**紧密结合**讨论。
